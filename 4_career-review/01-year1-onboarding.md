## The story of Year 1 (Apr 2021 — Mar 2022)

You joined the **UX1 squad** working on the createsend.com client-overview refresh. You came in with a small-fix cadence (recipients column, filter button moves, thumbnail-flashing fix) and within a month were owning full features end-to-end.

Three things stand out for Year 1:

**1. End-to-end feature ownership emerges quickly.** By mid-May you were shipping the **Bulk Delete Lists** feature including its feature flag (FF-538) — `UX1-437` was an "draw the rest of the owl" PR, the kind of thing you'd give a senior engineer. The **drag-and-drop custom fields table** (`UX1-474`) followed in May/June, which required upgrading react-beautiful-dnd and contributing back to the in-house **CMDS/cmds** design system. That's two design-system PRs ([CMDS/cmds#1875](https://git.campmon.com/CMDS/cmds/pull/1875), [CMDS/cmds#1895](https://git.campmon.com/CMDS/cmds/pull/1895)) in your first three months — a signal of broader trust.

**2. Multi-repo coordination becomes your move early.** `UX1-490` landed across **four repos in one week** — CMDS/cmds (cm-header package), Freshview/createsend.com (old master page), Freshview/TestingCore (consumer), Engineering/TestAutomation (test-package bump). That pattern — "this feature isn't done until it lands in every related service" — recurs every year after this.

**3. Squad transition is clean.** From August 2021 you start picking up Campaign Authoring (CA) work — `CA-982` "improved efficiency of re-importing HTML" first, then checklist nav-bar actions and key-actions tweaks. By January 2022 you're fully on CA-1188/1190/1191 work, and your reviews shift from UX1 partners (engineers on that squad) to CA partners (engineers on the Campaign Authoring squad). No half-finished UX1 work trailing behind.

The reviewer profile here is "JS-crew apprentice" — 87 reviews mostly inside createsend.com with a handful in TestAutomation and TestingCore. You're learning the codebase from both sides.
