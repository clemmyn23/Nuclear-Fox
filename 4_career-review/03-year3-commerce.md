## The story of Year 3 (Apr 2023 — Mar 2024)

The **Commerce / Automation Recipes squad** year. Shopify migration assistant + Commerce Management pages in H1, Automation Recipes in H2, and a clean late-year pivot into Journeys (AJ) that sets up the next two years.

What changed: the per-PR scope went up sharply. Year 3 has 84 authored PRs (down from 131 in Y2) but each PR is bigger and touches more services.

Three threads:

**1. The Shopify migration assistant / Commerce Management end-to-end.** `MER-394`/`MER-401`/`MER-491`/`MER-520`/`MER-536`/`MER-553`/`MER-723` landed across the monolith ([createsend.com#15341](https://git.campmon.com/Freshview/createsend.com/pull/15341), [#15434](https://git.campmon.com/Freshview/createsend.com/pull/15434), [#15631](https://git.campmon.com/Freshview/createsend.com/pull/15631)), protobuf contracts ([cm.protobuf#674](https://git.campmon.com/platform/cm.protobuf/pull/674)), the gRPC `ThirdPartyListImporter`, and HEX admin ([campmon.hex#813](https://git.campmon.com/Freshview/campmon.hex/pull/813)). This is your first "service mesh" feature — coordinating contract changes with backend and frontend on different release cadences.

**2. The Automation Recipes build-out.** `MER-451`/`MER-554`/`MER-616`/`MER-618`/`MER-635`/`MER-699` covered the single-recipe page, recipe overview/cataloging, recipe-creation analytics across SegmentTrackingPlan, agency-mode cleanups, and integration into the create-automation dialog. Cross-team work with several engineers evident from your review activity (top reviewees this year: an engineer with 13, another engineer with 9).

**3. Cross-service permission work (`MER-665`/`MER-668`/`MER-713`).** A single ticket landing across **four services in coordination**: [createsend.com#16221](https://git.campmon.com/Freshview/createsend.com/pull/16221) (monolith), [copyworkflowservice.core#19](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowcontext.copyworkflowservice.core/pull/19) (service), [copyworkflowservice-datastores#2](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowcontext.copyworkflowservice-datastores/pull/2) and [#3](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowcontext.copyworkflowservice-datastores/pull/3) (schema). The late-year AJ pivot ([workflowbuildercontext.uiservice.core#191](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowbuildercontext.uiservice.core/pull/191) AJ-194-a in March 2024) is interesting — it's just one PR in this year, but the reviewer activity already shifts toward EL deliverability + PP campaign-checklist by Q4, suggesting your team move was in motion before your authored PRs caught up.
