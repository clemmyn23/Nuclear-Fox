## The story of Year 4 (Apr 2024 — Mar 2025)

The **AJ tribe** year. Settled deep into Journeys/Automation work — UCF Advanced Expressions, Wait-Until step, Send-a-Test for journey email, condition-path swap, Create-segment-from-campaign-report. Plus a September 2024 detour to bootstrap **Bloom** as a greenfield prototype.

Year 4 numbers are smaller (66 authored, 84 reviewed) but the PRs are denser and the cross-repo coordination per-PR is higher. This is the "deep on one tribe" senior-IC year.

Three threads:

**1. UCF Advanced Expressions end-to-end (Apr–Oct 2024).** `AJ-301`/`AJ-311`/`AJ-332`/`AJ-336` — UI prototype, calculation editor, frontend validation, plan-tier gating, feature flag (`AdvancedExpressionsForUCF`, FF 945), unit tests, instrumented logging, and finally FF cleanup ([createsend.com#18316](https://git.campmon.com/Freshview/createsend.com/pull/18316), [workflowbuildercontext.uiservice.core#275](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowbuildercontext.uiservice.core/pull/275)). **2. Wait-Until step (Jul–Sep 2024).** `AJ-400`/`AJ-404`/`AJ-419` — DB schema and persistence (`AR_AutoresponderWaitUntilConfig`), action/config models with mappers, PUT endpoint handling update/delete, cross-DB clone for journey copy, tests, and CAL/GDPR scrubbing for sensitive fields. Followed up with the workflow support UI ([supportservice.core#41](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowcontext.supportservice.core/pull/41), [supportuiservice.core#23](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowcontext.supportuiservice.core/pull/23)) — including reviving and fixing the long-dormant frontend build.

**3. Bloom Bootstrap greenfield (Sept 2024).** Five PRs in [Deprecated/bloom-app](https://git.campmon.com/Deprecated/bloom-app) over two weeks: BTB-4 (docker compose), BTB-9 (AWS Cognito server-side), BTB-13 (compose watch), BTB-21 (change password). You set up DevX, auth, infra, and a basic UI from zero. The repos later moved to `Deprecated/` — a directional pivot, not abandoned work. Your reviews on this prototype suggest you were the tech-lead — three teammates all submitted to your reviewer team.

Other Year-4 work signals senior IC: **AJ-575** failure-reason handling across protobuf + runner service + monolith (a three-repo gRPC change in Feb 2025); **AJ-369** Terraform upgrade with DynamoDB PITR and deletion protection ([sampleeventpageservice.core#24](https://git.campmon.com/Engineering/workflowbuildercontext.sampleeventpageservice.core/pull/24)).
