## What changed between the two passes
- **"0 demos at showcases" collapsed.** The venue verifiably ran 4× in 12 months, was explicitly
  skipped at least once (29 Sep), and the series was deleted/recreated 4× — and one of the 4 runs
  fell during his leave *and featured his own feature*.
- **"Numbers in a private file" was overstated.** Adoption is monitored by PMs; the missing thing
  is a readout with his name on it — a smaller, different problem.
- **"Mentoring hides in DMs" stands** — now the only in-team visibility item left.

## The four moves (re-aimed at upward attribution)
1. **Take the presenter seat for your own features** — in the demo slot when it actually runs, and
   at the Weekly Technology Meeting, which runs every week and is where others currently narrate
   (Biome → Liam, AI-DLC → Luke). One WTM slot on JC-411 with the −69% number attaches it to you.
2. **Publish two bylined readouts**: Drip 90-days (with the PM, in #cm-product-updates) and the
   JC-411 before/after. Credit attaches to artefacts; there are currently none above the team.
3. **One-page build-state before any leave** (already committed in the SMS pack) — 27 Oct is the
   proof of what happens without it: your feature, someone else's microphone.
4. **In the 1:1, close the loop on the doc itself**: "Which of these do you see me missing, and
   what would you need to see, by when?" Bring the 360 doc's own words ("one of our key members").
   The critique is undocumented; make the criteria documented instead.
