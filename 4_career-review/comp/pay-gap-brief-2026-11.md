## Why this happened — it's mechanics, not merit
1. **Anchor:** your base was set in April 2021, pre-inflation, at a mid-level anchor — and every
   raise since has been a percentage of that anchor. Base compounds; yours compounds from 2021.
2. **No promotion has ever re-based it.** Title still "Software Engineer" after 5.6 years while the
   evidence record (files 15/16) puts the work at Senior → Staff-trending. Promo/comp has been
   frozen post-acquisition — your own notes call it frozen, and the CM ladder has no visible cycle.
3. **Salary compression/inversion:** newer hires are priced at the market of their hire year.
   Anyone hired or re-hired 2023–2026 starts above a 2021 anchor plus frozen increments. This is
   the single most common reason a stronger engineer earns less than newer peers — it's structural,
   and it never self-corrects; it only corrects through a re-banding event (promotion, counter,
   or a new job).
4. **Invisible-scope discount:** your biggest 2026 contributions (SMS TL-in-practice, launch
   readiness, mentoring 4 engineers, −69% CI compute) are exactly the kinds of work that don't
   re-price anyone automatically — they re-price you only if a manager takes a case to a cycle.
   There is no cycle, and nobody has taken the case.
5. **You never had a competing anchor.** No counter-offer, no re-negotiation event in 5.6 years.
   Companies re-price at hire and at threat-of-loss; you've presented neither.

**What it is NOT:** underperformance. The CADD evidence pack
([cadd-self-review-2026-11.md](../cadd-self-review-2026-11.md),
[findings-impact-numbers.md](../data/2026-h2/findings-impact-numbers.md)) shows delivery, adoption
numbers, and multiplier effects that peers' records don't match. If performance set pay, the
ordering would be inverted.

## The move (in order of leverage)
1. **Internal, once, in writing:** after the CADD lands, ask for a comp review anchored on scope:
   "I'm operating as tech lead on SMS and Drip with measured outcomes (688 accounts, −69% CI
   compute, 4 mentees). I'd like my compensation and title reviewed against that scope this cycle.
   What would it take, and by when?" Name market ($160–180k senior band per your market brief),
   never peers. Get the answer, and its date, in writing.
2. **Set the deadline in your head, not out loud.** If the answer is "no cycle / case-by-case /
   next year", that is the freeze confirming itself — your notes already model what staying costs
   (~$300k over 5 yrs, [cost-of-staying-model.md](cost-of-staying-model.md)).
3. **The market re-prices you in one step.** The interview prep, résumé and target list in this
   folder exist for exactly this. A signed offer is the only lever that has ever force-corrected
   compression — use it to leave or, if you'd stay, to re-anchor.
