## Universal rules
- **One screen, max.** Hiring managers skim. The cover letter is 150 words, not 400.
- **The differentiator goes in paragraph one or two** — your IC+leadership combo and the SMASH scope. It's the thing they'll remember.
- **Always one bespoke sentence about *them*** — generic letters get binned. 5 minutes of homework per company.
- **Never paste your current salary.** If a form *requires* a number, put your target ($170k) or "negotiable / open."
- **Match the résumé variant** (`cv/resume-variants.md`) to the role and reference its framing in the letter.
- No invented metrics — "zero critical failures in five years," "25-person team," "8 years" are all real; don't dress them up with fake percentages.
