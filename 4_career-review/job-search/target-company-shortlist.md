# Target-Company Shortlist — Sydney

Grouped by category, with *why each fits you*. Your stack (.NET/C# + TypeScript/React + gRPC/Kafka/MSSQL) and your IC-plus-leadership combo are the lenses. **This is a research starting point, not a claim about who's hiring or what they pay** — validate everything.

## A. Top-tier product / big tech (highest total comp; equity)
Best if you want the biggest comp reset and don't mind a tougher loop.
- **Atlassian** — huge eng org, strong levelling/comp rigour, real IC ladder (anti-dote to your freeze). Polyglot, but values system design + leadership.
- **Canva** — Sydney's marquee scale-up; product eng at scale; strong comp.
- **Google / Amazon / Microsoft (Sydney)** — big comp, formal levelling; expect heavier algo + system-design prep (your CRM-Lite + SMASH designs help).
- **SafetyCulture** — Sydney product company, well-regarded eng culture, scale-up comp.
- *Fit note:* lead with the Staff-IC résumé variant + both system-design stories; comp here often clears $180k+ total for senior.

## B. Strong .NET / C# shops (your home stack — fastest technical fit)
You'll interview *with the wind at your back* where C#/.NET is first-class.
- **Octopus Deploy** — .NET-centric, strong engineering brand, remote-friendly from Australia. Excellent stack match.
- **WiseTech Global** — your alma mater; C#/MSSQL core; you know the codebase culture (boomerang hires are common and you'd re-enter at a higher level than "Associate").
- **Tyro / Zeller** — payments, .NET + modern stack, Sydney.
- **SiteMinder** — Sydney travel-SaaS, mixed stack incl. .NET.
- *Fit note:* the Backend/Platform variant shines here; your gRPC/Kafka/event-driven work is directly relevant.

## C. Banks & finance (stable comp, less churn — antidote to C-suite chaos)
If leadership stability and predictable pay matter after the last five years.
- **Macquarie** — strong eng pay, .NET + modern; demanding but stable.
- **Commonwealth Bank (CommBank / x15)** — large eng org, defined levels.
- **Westpac / NAB**, **Afterpay/Block** — varied stacks; bank comp is base-heavy (good for cashflow certainty).
- *Fit note:* banks reward the "reliability + leadership" story (your SMASH zero-failure record reads well); levelling tends to be more formal = less of your under-levelling risk.

## D. Mid-size product / scale-ups (scope + leadership runway)
Where your IC+leadership combo can land you a **Lead/Staff/EM** title sooner.
- **Culture Amp, Employment Hero, Linktree, Airwallex, Deputy, Mr Yum, Prospa, Flare** — Sydney/Melbourne scale-ups; smaller orgs mean your 25-person leadership record is *directly* a lead/EM credential.
- *Fit note:* lead with the EM/Tech-Lead variant; these are the places most likely to value both halves of you at once.

## E. Events / ticketing-adjacent (your unfair advantage)
You have *rare, real* domain expertise here from SMASH.
- **Humanitix, Moshtix, Ticketek/TEG, Eventbrite (AU), Audience Republic** — your founding-engineer ticketing/scanning build + live-ops record is a genuine differentiator almost no other SWE candidate has.
- *Fit note:* this is the one category where your volunteer work is a *headline*, not a footnote.

## F. Marketing/email/SaaS (lateral — you know the domain cold)
- Competitors/adjacents to Campaign Monitor: **ActiveCampaign (AU), Klaviyo (AU presence), Brevo, HubSpot (AU), Intercom (Dublin/remote)** — you'd ramp instantly given domain familiarity.
- *Fit note:* domain expertise shortens time-to-impact; use it as a "why me" angle, but watch for the same SaaS-churn risks you're leaving (screen with the reverse-interview questions).

## How to work this list
1. **Pick 8–12 across categories** (don't over-index on one). Aim for a mix that lets offers land together (leverage — see the negotiation script).
2. **Match résumé variant to category:** Staff-IC → A/B; Backend → B; EM/Lead → D; either → C/E/F.
3. **Screen every one** with `interview/reverse-interview-questions.md` — especially A and F, where churn risk is real.
4. **Warm intros beat cold applies** — check LinkedIn for anyone in your network (incl. ex-CM/ex-WiseTech colleagues) at these.
5. Track it all in `job-search/tracker.csv`.
